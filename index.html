Superfun · pitch round

Five daily word games

Each one built around the two feelings that actually make people post a result every morning: I was clever, or I was so close.

Chains

Compound golf

Get from FIRE to CAR in four words. No undo.

Every word must form a compound with the last. Three holes, a par, and no take-backs — so a bad link strands you in the rough for real.

Play → · 3 holes · ~3 min

The Rule

Deduction

Six words. Three pass, three fail. Why?

Probe with any word — real or nonsense — until you can name the hidden rule. Then call it. You get one call.

Play → · 6 probes · ~2 min

Shunt

Tactile grid

Slide the rows. Slide the columns. Spell four words at once.

Fixing the bottom row breaks the top one. Multiple solutions exist — and the game tells you how rare yours was.

Play → · par 6 · ~3 min

Seed

Growth · strategy

EAT → EATS → TEARS → ORATES → …

One letter arrives each turn from a queue everyone can see coming. Use every letter, every time. It isn't anagramming — it's planning.

Play → · 9 letters · ~3 min

Stowaways

Hidden in prose

Six themed words hiding in two ordinary sentences.

Not a grid — a scene. The words span the gaps between real words, camouflaged by meaning, and your eye reads straight past them.

Play → · 6 finds · ~3 min
